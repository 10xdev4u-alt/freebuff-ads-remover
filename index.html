Freebuff Ads Remover

Pluggable extension that removes every ad from Freebuff Desktop — with a live shield dashboard counting every stop.

v1.2.0 · MIT · Linux AppImage Get Started

Network Blocking

19 ad and tracking endpoints cancelled at the Electron session layer before they leave the app.

Shield Dashboard

In-app badge and panel: blocks per funnel stage, top surfaces, latest stops, DOM-hide counter.

Sponsored Cards

Server-rendered in-chat sponsor cards hidden by their real markup — no keywords to dodge.

Update Survival

The launcher adopts newer updater payloads automatically. Restart from the icon after an update and the shield is back.

How It Works

Electron ignores --require for packaged apps, so the launcher patches the app payload instead — then the extension loads before the app boots.
